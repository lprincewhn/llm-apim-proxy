## Interpretation and restored state

Management deletion did not immediately stop data-plane success. After the
first 404, successes and failures alternated. Error accumulation, ingestion and
evaluation contribute to the first-error-to-alert interval. The write-to-response
interval includes propagation, request cadence and request duration, not just
gateway propagation. Two short probes and 13 successes do not prove sustained
capacity, a percentile latency target or a maximum failover duration.

East US 2 was subsequently restored with its original GlobalStandard capacity
150 and `gpt-5.1` model version `2025-11-13`. The final report records
`primary=sweden`, `enabled=["sweden","eastus2"]`, `version=9`, both alerts enabled
and `switchEnabled=true`; the issue result reports both backends healthy.
The standby was restored manually without failback. These values supersede
the earlier Sweden-only/version-2 snapshot, not future operator changes.

The earlier deletion attempt (119 successful requests over approximately
10m 05s) did not produce a measurable fault/switch. The 09:17 executor-era
drill is separate historical evidence. Current-topology latency-triggered
failover, SSE interruption handling, Java/Search 8s/15s targets, N-1 capacity
and production HA remain separate acceptance work.
